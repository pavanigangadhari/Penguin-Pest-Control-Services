FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Penguin Pest Control Services.

What is pest control?

Pest control is the professional management and treatment of unwanted pests such as termites, cockroaches, rodents, ants, mosquitoes and bed bugs.

Do you provide anti-termite treatment for new buildings?

Yes. We provide pre-construction anti-termite treatment solutions for suitable residential and commercial construction projects.

Do you install termite pipes?

Yes. We provide termite pipe installation for suitable construction projects, along with required fittings and accessories.

Do you provide pest control for existing buildings?

Yes. We provide post-construction pest control and anti-termite treatment for existing properties.

Is pest control safe?

Safety depends on the chemical, application method, property conditions and proper usage. Treatment is carried out according to the product label and applicable safety requirements. Customers should follow the instructions provided by the technician after treatment.

How much does pest control cost?

The cost depends on the pest, property size, infestation level, treatment method and location. Contact us for a customized quotation.

Do you provide commercial pest control?

Yes. We provide pest-management solutions for offices, shops, warehouses, restaurants, commercial buildings and industrial facilities.

Do you provide services in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana?

Yes. Penguin Pest Control Services provides services across selected locations in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, including Hyderabad and surrounding areas.

How can I get a quotation?

Contact us by phone or WhatsApp and share your property location, approximate area and pest problem. We can then discuss the appropriate treatment and quotation.
